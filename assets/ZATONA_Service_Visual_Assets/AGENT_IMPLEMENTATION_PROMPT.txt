ZATONA WEBSITE — SERVICES, VISUAL ASSETS & INTERACTION IMPLEMENTATION

Use the ZATONA Service Visual Assets package and the approved ZATONA Price Proposal PDF as the source of truth.

1. Inspect the existing project before editing. Identify its HTML/CSS/JS architecture, deployment base path, language switcher, RTL/LTR handling, and design tokens. Do not rebuild the website from scratch.

2. Read the package README and use this exact image mapping:
- 01-social-media-management.webp → Social Media Management & Digital Marketing
- 02-customer-support.webp → Customer Support
- 03-paid-advertising.webp → Paid Advertising / Campaign Management
- 04-graphic-design.webp → Graphic Design
- 05-cv-and-linkedin.webp → CV & Career Profile Services
- 06-photo-video-production.webp → Optional field photography / short-video add-ons only

3. Integrate images into the Services section. Use the first five as primary service-card visuals. Use the sixth only in a clearly labeled optional add-ons area or related detail. Do not use one generic image for every service. Keep consistent image ratios, restrained shadows, rounded corners, and responsive sizing. Preserve ZATONA's olive/cream identity. Add meaningful Arabic and English alt text. Lazy-load below-the-fold images and reserve their aspect ratios to prevent layout shifts.

4. Improve the service-card system:
- Distinct title and concise accurate description.
- Optional small category label.
- Clear “Learn more” / “اطلب الخدمة” CTA.
- Consistent card heights and spacing.
- Balanced 2–3 column desktop grid depending on viewport; single-column or clean stacked mobile layout.
- Keep visuals meaningful but not oversized.
- Do not turn service cards into pricing tables. Link to the relevant pricing/detail section.

5. Preserve proposal details exactly; do not invent or silently alter prices, quantities, inclusions, guarantees, clients, testimonials, or results:
A. Social media management & digital marketing: monthly packages EGP 4,500 / 8,500 / 15,000. Platforms, post counts, designs, reels, scheduling, comment handling, paid campaign management, reporting and meetings differ by tier.
B. Customer support: monthly packages EGP 6,500 / 12,500 / 24,000. Coverage is 4 / 8 / 16 hours daily; working days 6 / 6 / 7 days weekly; included conversations up to 600 / 1,800 / 5,000 monthly. Preserve channel and target response-time distinctions.
C. Paid ads management fees: EGP 3,000 monthly up to EGP 20,000 ad spend; EGP 5,500 monthly for EGP 20,001–60,000; 10% of budget for EGP 60,001–150,000; above that by agreement. State clearly that ad spend is paid directly by the client to advertising platforms and is not included in management fees.
D. Graphic design: preserve individual and bundle prices listed in the proposal.
E. CV services: preserve CV, bilingual CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile prices listed in the proposal.
F. Add-ons: additional platform, additional 10 posts, additional short video and field photography.

6. Add polished interaction and micro-interactions:
- Primary and secondary buttons need tasteful hover transitions.
- On hover: subtle lift (small translateY), slight shadow increase, and restrained background/text color transition.
- Add visible :focus-visible states for keyboard users.
- Add pressed feedback with :active.
- Service cards can subtly lift or change border/shadow on hover; their images may scale slightly, without cropping important details.
- Add restrained reveal-on-scroll for headings/cards only if it does not delay content; use IntersectionObserver and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Add active navigation state and smooth anchor scrolling, respecting reduced-motion.
- Touch users must have access to all content and actions without hover.
- Avoid excessive bouncing, cursor-following effects, parallax, or distracting animation.

7. Accessibility and performance:
- Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and sufficient contrast.
- Avoid heavy animation libraries for simple effects.
- Prefer CSS transforms and opacity for animation.
- Content must remain visible if JavaScript fails.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

8. Responsive and bilingual QA:
Test at 1440px, 1366px, 1024px, 768px, and 390px widths.
Check Arabic RTL and English LTR, image order, card alignment, wrapping, CTA placement, and horizontal overflow.

9. Final report:
List files changed, exact images and paths used, proposal-vs-website conflicts, remaining design limitations, and responsive/accessibility checks. Do not commit or push without approval.
